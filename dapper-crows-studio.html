Dapper crows studio

A murder of crows on telegraph wires, all dressed for the occasion: top hats, bowlers, fezzes and boaters, bow ties and cravats, a monocle and chain here and there, waistcoats in pinstripe, argyle or herringbone. Export a millimetre SVG with three pen layers for Design Space.

Split export saves one SVG per pen (black outline, grey feathers, colour cloth), numbered in plotting order. All carry the same four corner ticks so Design Space sizes them identically: place each file at the same spot on the mat, swap pens between runs, and leave the paper on the mat.

computing

Every crow is one outline, a pear-shaped body with its head, a fanned tail, a folded wing, a beak and two feet that wrap the wire, built from a few dozen control points. Each part hides the lines behind it, so the hat sits over the head, the wing over the waistcoat and a crow on a lower wire stands in front of the one above. Cloth patterns (pinstripe, gingham, argyle, herringbone, polka dots, regimental stripes) are clipped to the waistcoat, cravat, bow tie and hatband. The feathers are hatched in the second pen and darkened with a cross layer on the back and belly. In Design Space: three layers to Pen, black 0.4 for the outline, black 0.3 (or grey) for the feathers, a colour pen for the cloth, Attach, Make.
