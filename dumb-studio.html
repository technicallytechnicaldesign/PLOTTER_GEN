Dumb studio

A dumb terminal with DUMB on its screen, peering over the top of its own case at you as if you were the dumb one. The screen is a real 1-bit framebuffer: its scanlines are the hatching, and the lit pixels are left as bare paper, so the word is negative space. Sticky note, PEBKAC, ID-10-T, and a two-panel gag. Export a millimetre SVG with three pen layers for Design Space.

Paper and pens

The screen is drawn by the black pen as scanlines, so the more pixels across and scanlines per pixel, the darker and longer it plots; lines never go closer than 0.4 mm. The colour pen draws the phosphor (if you ask for it), the iris rings, the power light, the sticky note and the ANY key. Split export saves one SVG per pen, numbered in plotting order, all with the same four corner ticks so Design Space sizes them identically.

computing

The terminal is a solid in oblique projection: a front shell and a tapering tube housing, and only the faces turned toward you get their edges drawn. Everything behind something else (the eyes behind the case, the plinth behind the keyboard, the bezel under the note) is trimmed against the outline of what is in front. The screen is a grid of pixels; every pixel row is crossed by one to four scanlines, and the lit pixels are the gaps. In Design Space: three layers to Pen, black 0.4 for outlines and the screen, black 0.3 (or grey) for the hatching, the colour pen last, Attach, Make.
