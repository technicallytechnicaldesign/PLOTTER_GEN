Kiwi studio

Flightless, and proud of it: shaggy brown kiwi drawn in hair-fine feather strokes, standing alone under the ferns and the Southern Cross, stacked into a wobbly tower, tiled into wallpaper or packed in until the page gives up. A kiwifruit sometimes sneaks in. Day 18 of Inktober, "flightless".

How it is made: each kiwi is one smooth body outline (a pear with a small head), roughened into hair-like tufts that flick back away from the head, then filled with short feather strokes that hang down and back, thicker underneath. The long beak curves down, with whiskers at its base and nostrils at the very tip, where a kiwi keeps them. Legs are stout and scaled, three toes forward and one back. Everything in front cuts the lines behind it, so a kiwi standing on another's back stands in its fluff.
