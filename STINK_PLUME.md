# Stink plume

**UID:** PLG-STK-6F31879F · **Version:** 1 · 2026-10-08

Inktober day 8, prompt "stinky". `stink-plume-studio.html`: the smell is the drawing. Something stinky sits at the bottom of the sheet and its smell rises off it as pen lines, with flies looping through the fog.

## Sources

Corpse flower (titan arum in a pot), cheese (holey or blue) on a board, an old sock that stands up by itself, a fish or a fishbone on a plate, a dustbin with its lid up.

## Smell styles

- **Plume:** evenly spaced streamlines (Jobard-Lefer) through a field that rises straight off the source, then turns turbulent with height (curl noise). Spacing grows with height, so the smell thins out as it climbs; the top can break into dashes.
- **Cartoon:** the classic comic stink lines: a few wavy wisps that curl over at the end.
- **Smell map:** contour lines of a smell concentration field, a chain of growing puffs, swirled by the same noise.

Smell lines stop at the source and the flies, and never come closer than 0.6 mm (checked as a gate).

## Pens

Line 0.4 (the source, flies), shade 0.3 (hatching, fly trails), smell 0.4 in a colour pen of your choice.
