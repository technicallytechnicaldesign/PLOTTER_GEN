Gear BAM! studio

Solid 3D gears meshing under a comic sound effect. Walls are shaded with crosshatching that follows the teeth, tops with a riso halftone that can sit a little off register, and a jagged burst and chunky extruded letters go off where the teeth bite. Three pen layers, all plottable.

Split export saves one SVG per pen that has line (outline, hatch, dots), numbered in plotting order. Each carries the same four corner ticks so Design Space sizes them identically: place every file at the same spot on the mat, swap pens between runs, and leave the paper on the mat.

computing

Gears are the spur_gear_goblin_v0_6.scad sections from gear-reverb-studio.html (teeth, keyed bore, web pocket drawn as engraved rings), meshing with the SCAD's pair phase, extruded and seen through an orthographic camera. Hidden lines come from a painter's raster at 6 px per mm: every face is filled into an ID buffer back to front, outlines are traced where the owner changes, and every hatch line, dot and crease is kept only where its own face won. Three layers in the SVG: black 0.4 for outlines, black 0.3 for hatching and speed lines, and a spot layer of spiral halftone dots for any pen you like (a coloured pen is the point). In Design Space: all three to Pen, Attach, Make.
