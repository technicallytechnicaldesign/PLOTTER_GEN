computing

How it is made: a real Enigma (rotors I to V, reflector B, plugboard, double stepping) is built in the page. For the Bombe, the crib is enciphered with the key, the pairs of letters become the menu, and every drum is turned to the letter it shows when the machine stops on the right answer, offset by its place on the menu. For the Wren, the message is enciphered letter by letter and laid across the panel row by row, and each letter's size and pen come from how dark the portrait is under it. The key is printed underneath, and the studio checks every build by decoding the letters on the sheet back to the message.
