# Mystical

**Version:** 1 · 2026-10-09

Inktober day 10, prompt "mystical". One studio, `mystical-studio.html`: pick something mystical and a pair of hands to do something with it, then plot, cut and layer the pieces so the hands really do hold it.

## The three pieces

Every scene is up to three pieces of card, all drawn on one page at one scale, so each cut-out drops back where it was drawn.

1. **The backdrop.** An arched window (or a rounded card) with a hatched night sky, thicker at the top, stars, and a row of moon phases. The sky is left bare under the pieces that sit on it, except for a 1.5 mm rim, so a slightly crooked cut-out still hides the gap.
2. **The object.** A crystal ball, tarot cards or a tea cup.
3. **The hands.** A pair, each with a bell sleeve, drawn as paper hands: fingers bend in the plane of the page.

"This sheet" chooses what the page holds: everything together (one flat picture) or a single piece with its cut line. On a lower sheet a dashed guide, just inside the edge of the next piece up, shows where it goes. It is covered once glued. Foam pads between layers give the depth. "Export every piece" saves the three sheets in one go.

## The objects

- **Crystal ball.** A crescent-moon or pedestal stand, shaded glass with a window glint, and something in it: drifting mist (streamlines of a slow noise field), a constellation (the shortest tree joining the stars, less its longest line), an eye, the moon, a galaxy, or an answer in words (YES, NO, SOON, WAIT, NOT YET, TRUST IT, LOOK AGAIN, ASK LATER, picked by the seed).
- **Tarot cards.** One card, a row, or a fan, 3 to 7 cards. Eight pictures (sun, moon, star, wheel, eye, key, cup, sword) with a numeral and a name in the stroke font; the middle card can carry a name of your own. Cards can be face up, backs (a lattice and a star), or mixed.
- **Tea cup.** Cup and saucer seen from a little above, a handle on either side (or none), a band of signs round the bowl, steam on the backdrop, and the leaves left in the shape of a heart, crescent, star, ring, spiral or wave.

## The hands

- Each hand is a palm, three bones to a finger and two to a thumb, each bone a tapered capsule. Their union is rasterised at 0.25 mm; the outline, the cut line and the rounded webs between the fingers all come from that. Each finger, the thumb and the palm are separate items in a back-to-front stack, so one finger can lie over another.
- **Poses:** open, fan, reach, claw, hook, cradle, point, pinch, grip, hold. Each scene's menu lists what makes sense for its object: hover over the glass, cradle it, conjure, one points; hold the fan, pinch a card, point at a card, hover over the spread; hold the cup by its handle, cradle the bowl, cradle it and point at the leaves, hover over the steam; palm reading, an open palm, two palms.
- **Witchy detail:** nails 0 to 16 mm past the fingertip (almond, stiletto, coffin, round, square, claw), filled with a polish pen, with an optional gold moon or star; rings; a mark on the back of the hand (eye, sun, moon and stars, star, constellation, henna). Palm side: the heart, head, life, fate and sun lines, and the planet signs on the mounts.
- **Sleeves:** dark and hatched, lace with a scalloped hem, or plain. A sleeve is shortened on its own, and the whole group drawn a little smaller, if something would run off the page.

## Pens and cuts

Gold, violet, polish, the shade pen, the line pen, and a cut layer. Split export saves one SVG per pen, each with the same four corner ticks. The cut line sits 1 to 5 mm outside the ink and rounds off the nail tips. A gate reports how much of a piece is thinner than 2.4 mm.

## Not tested yet

Pen widths, the narrowest cut the knife will hold (nail tips, the sleeve scallops), and whether the pieces register on paper. A first proof of all three sheets, cut and stacked, will say.
