# Victorian lady

**Version:** 1 · 2026-10-11

Inktober day 14, prompt "lady". One studio, `victorian-lady-studio.html`: a fashion-plate paper doll with a wardrobe, drawn in ink and cut from card.

## What it makes

- **The doll:** one flat piece with an hourglass figure (full bust, laced waist, rounded hips, long neck), in chemise, corset (busk, gussets, boning), drawers with a knee frill, striped stockings and buttoned boots, with lidded eyes, lashes, arched brows and bow lips. Her hair is a sculpted silhouette with fine etched strands in the grey pen: a pompadour with a spiral top coil and a jewelled comb, ringlets with spiral curls cascading to the shoulders, or a braided coronet with ear coils.
- **The base:** a cross plate, not a foot tab. A 1.2 mm notch runs up 15 mm between her legs from a flat sole, a matching notch runs down 15 mm from the top of the base, and the two interlock at right angles so she stands on her own.
- **Gowns, three periods:** crinoline 1860 (dome skirt, tiers, pagoda sleeves), bustle 1885 (long cuirass bodice, swagged apron, pleated flounce), leg-o'-mutton 1895 (balloon sleeves, bell skirt, braid band). Each as a day dress, an evening gown (off the shoulder, bertha lace, corsage) or a walking suit (skirt, blouse and jacket as three pieces).
- **Under and over:** a petticoat piece (hoops, bustle pad or flounces), a fringed capelet, a cameo choker.
- **Hats:** poke bonnet, toque with a plume, picture hat, boater; all four on one hat sheet.
- **Held things:** parasol, fan, reticule, muff on a cord, bouquet, one per hand.
- **Cloth and trim:** plain, stripes, check, dots, tucks, damask lattice; colour fill none, light or rich; a trim dial for lace, bows, rosettes and buttons. Four pens (black, grey shade, cloth colour, trim colour) plus the cut.

## How it hangs together

- Garments are cut to her outline grown by 1.1 mm. Dresses and jackets hang from two shoulder tabs (straps, off the shoulder), skirts from two waist tabs, things in a hand from a band across the wrist with a tab each side.
- Hats tie on with ribbon strings that run down the cheek and fold behind at the jaw.
- Every tab is found by walking out from inside her outline until it leaves her edge, then standing 5 mm free past the garment's own edge.
- Round 2 (2026-10-11, maker's notes): the foot-tab bar stand was replaced by the interlocking cross base; the figure, face and hair were redrawn.
- Gowns are fitted to this doll's measurements (height, waist, arm pose). Change height or waist and cut the gown again.

## Gates (every build)

One closed outline per piece, two tabs standing free on every garment, no ink within 0.4 mm of a cut line, pieces 3 mm apart and inside the margin, pen length under 150 m, trim frame equal to the corner ticks. MEASURED: all 17 shipped sheets pass; 200 random settings pass, a build takes 0.4 to 1.2 s.
