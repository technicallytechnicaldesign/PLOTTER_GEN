Miniature kit

Inktober day 3, miniature. The Meatsack figure comes as a model kit, parts and augments still on the sprue. One part is a chip, and a loupe over it shows what the chip really is: an isometric city where the figure's memory, language, habits and fears are kept, with a cathedral core at the centre. Somewhere in that city someone is holding a loupe of their own.

Instructions
Ink

Dark areas are hatched, never filled solid: 0.75 mm crosshatch read as an even dark tone on paper, and under 0.5 mm the lines flood and soak the paper. Shaded faces of the city are crosshatched, lit faces carry windows.

Pens
Paper

Split export saves one SVG per pen in plotting order: colours first, then the thin pen, then the black line last so it sits on top. Each file carries four corner ticks at 1 mm, so they all land in the same place.

computing

How it is made: the kit is one distance field, frame, runners, gates and parts merged, so it outlines the way a moulded sprue does. The parts use the Meatsack studio's body proportions (head units), packed in rows. The city is an isometric die drawn front to back: every building's outline and hatching is clipped by the ones already drawn in front of it. It is then cut to the lens and pushed through a bulge that magnifies the middle and squeezes the rim, the way glass does. Pens are 0.4 mm for the line and colours and 0.3 mm for the thin pen (ASSUMED), none plotted yet.
