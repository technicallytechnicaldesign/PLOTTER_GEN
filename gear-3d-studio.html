§3 gear 3d studio / floor patterns

Solid SCAD gears standing on a floor, hidden lines removed, seen through a lens you can widen or flatten. The floor carries a pattern set off by the gears: spark rays, reverb rings, ripple interference, a stream bent round the spinning gears, or ribbon Truchet tiles, and a second pattern can sit on its own pen.

Split export saves one SVG per pen that has line (gears, floor, second floor, hatch, dots), numbered in plotting order, each with the same four corner ticks so Design Space sizes them alike. Place every file at the same spot on the mat and swap pens between runs.

computing

Gears are the spur_gear_goblin_v0_6.scad sections (teeth, keyed bore, web pocket as engraved rings) from gear-reverb-studio.html, meshing with the SCAD pair phase; hidden lines come from the same painter's ID buffer as gear-bam-studio.html, now under a perspective lens. Floor patterns are built in the floor plane, then projected: every point of every floor line is checked against the local spacing it lands at on paper, and dropped where neighbouring lines would sit closer than the tightest gap, so patterns fade out into the distance instead of clogging. Spark rays follow gear_reverb_3d.py --bg rays (one dashed ray out of each tooth tip, stopping at the next gear); reverb rings follow --bg rings.
