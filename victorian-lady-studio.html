Paper Dolls / Paper Clothes

A fashion-plate lady to cut and dress: one flat doll in her corset, drawers and boots, on a stand, with gowns from three decades (crinoline 1860, bustle 1885, leg-o'-mutton 1895), a walking suit, an evening gown, and hats, parasols, fans and wraps. Everything is flat card that hangs on her with fold tabs behind the shoulders, the waist and the hands, and the hats tie on with ribbon strings.

Paper and pens

Split export saves one SVG per layer in job order: the pens (black outline and detail, grey shading, cloth colour, trim colour), then the cut. The pen files carry four corner ticks at 1 mm and the cut file carries a trim rectangle at 1 mm.

computing

How it goes together: the doll is one flat piece with a tab under her boots that slides into a slot in the stand. Every garment is cut to her outline grown by about a millimetre, with fold tabs that stick out past her edge (over the shoulders, at the waist, round the hands) and fold behind her. A dress hangs from two shoulder tabs, a skirt from two waist tabs. Hats have two ribbon strings that run down the cheeks and fold behind at the jaw. Things held in a hand have a band across the wrist with a tab each side. The gowns are built from measurements of the doll's outline, so they only fit this doll at this height and waist: change either and cut the gown again. All cut sizes (tab width 7 mm, slot 1.2 mm, the thinnest card bridge) are assumptions until the cut calibration sheet has been read.
