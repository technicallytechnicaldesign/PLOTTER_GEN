Pens and paper

Three pens, plotted in this order: the thin pen (hatching, centre lines, dimensions, lettering), the black line pen (the parts), then the colour pen (turning arrows, revision clouds, stamps and the markups nobody asked for). Each split file carries the same four corner ticks.

computing

How it is made: the chain solver starts from the driver and grows one link at a time. A gear mesh puts the new shaft at exactly the sum of the two pitch radii, with the new gear turned so a tooth faces a gap. A belt takes the outer tangents of its two pulleys. Each link is kept only if it clears everything already placed. Every shaft carries its real turning speed, so the arrows and the ratio in the notes are true. Plausibility breaks things on purpose (mismatched teeth, square teeth, knotted or twisted belts, a shaft held up by optimism). Bureaucracy adds dimensions, balloons, revisions, stamps and notes.
