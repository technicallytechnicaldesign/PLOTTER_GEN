Gear weave studio

A crowd of gears, twenty to four hundred, laid so they overlap and then woven: every rim goes under its neighbour on one side and over it on the other, like chainmail or a knot drawing. Export a millimetre SVG with two pen layers for Design Space.

Split export saves one SVG per pen (outline, then fill), numbered in plotting order. Both carry the same four corner ticks so Design Space sizes them identically: place each file at the same spot on the mat, swap pens between runs, and leave the paper on the mat.

computing

Tooth outlines are the involute teeth from gear-reverb-studio.html (the port of spur_gear_goblin_v0_6.scad, checked against OpenSCAD), at any tooth count from 10 up. The weave rule is one line: where two gears overlap, a gear is on top on the left of the line to its neighbour and underneath on the right, so going round any rim it alternates under, over, under. Rims always pass over spokes and hubs. Where three gears pile up the rule can disagree with itself and a small patch drops out; the stats count those points. In Design Space: both layers to Pen, black 0.4 on the outlines, black 0.3 (or a colour) on the fill, Attach, Make.
