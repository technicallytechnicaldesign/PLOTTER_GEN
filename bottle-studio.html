Bottle studio

A small apothecary for the plotter. Pick a scene (one bottle, a shelf of many, or a potions countertop), pick the bottle shapes and the label, and it draws glass, liquid, stopper and label as clean pen lines. The label text is yours, and it starts out as "Rescue Remedy" for the Inktober prompt "rescue".

Glass and liquid
Whimsy

Whimsy leans bottles by up to 3 degrees, wobbles the glass by up to 4 percent and tips the labels a little. Zero is a catalogue plate.

Pens
Paper

Split export saves one SVG per pen in plotting order: the colour liquid first, then the thin pen, then the black line last so it sits on top. Each file carries four corner ticks so they all land in the same place.

computing

How it is made: every bottle is a half-profile (bottom corner, body, shoulder, neck, lip) mirrored into an outline, so the seven shapes are seven small sets of numbers. Liquid, glass shading and the surface are hatch lines clipped to the inside of that outline, and the label is cut out of them so the pen never crosses the paper. Lettering is single-stroke, two passes 0.28 mm apart, laid along the gentle curve of the wrap.
