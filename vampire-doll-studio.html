Vampire paper doll

A jointed cut-out vampire for spooky season: fangs, a widow's peak, pointed ears, a tailcoat with a bow tie and a medallion, and a cape with a tall collar that rides on the shoulder pins behind the body. The pen draws each part, the blade cuts them out with a pin hole at every joint, and brass split pins put it together. The body is the same doll as the jointed figure studio, so the paper doll wardrobe's clothes fit it too.

Paper and pens

Split export saves one SVG per layer in job order: the pen layers, then the cut. The pen files carry four corner ticks at 1 mm and the cut file carries a trim rectangle at 1 mm, so every file lands in the same place in Design Space.

computing

How it goes together: each part is drawn as a set of rounded shapes merged into one outline, and every joint is a disc of card round a pin hole that sits at exactly the same point on both parts, so a brass split pin goes through both. The cape is cut with three holes that line up with the shoulder pins (A and B) and the neck pin (N): stack the cape at the back, then the body, then the arms and head in front, and push each pin through everything. The arms then swing in front of the cape while it hangs still. The assembly letters say which holes meet. For a jumping jack, the arms and legs go behind the body and in front of the cape, and one thread ties across the four small string holes. Cut sizes (pin holes, the 3 mm of card round them, the 5 mm narrowest part) are assumed until the cut calibration sheet is measured.
