Fracture studio

Reality, cracked like a pane of glass: an engraved world of hills, a cottage and a wanderer, broken from an impact into shards. Loose shards drift apart, some are rasterised into halftone pixels, the innermost are gone and show what was always behind the picture: its own wireframe, falling code or a circuit board, in the colour pen. Bits leak out on the wind, past the frame, and glitch bands slip the world sideways. Export a millimetre SVG with three pen layers for Design Space.

A tear runs its seam down through Impact across and gapes widest at Impact down; Breach, Pull apart and Rasterise act on the crumbs along its edges. Split export saves one SVG per pen (black for the world and its cracks, grey for the hatching and the pixels, colour for what is behind it and the leaking bits), numbered in plotting order. All carry the same four corner ticks so Design Space sizes them identically.

computing

The world is drawn first, back to front, each hill hiding what lies behind it, then broken: shards are cells crowded toward each impact, their shared edges jagged the same way from both sides, so every shard is a real polygon. Near the breach the cracks lose their resolution and step along the pixel grid. Each shard keeps, loses or rasterises the lines that fell inside it, and moves as one piece, so its hatching stays whole. What shows through the gaps is clipped to the gaps, a little inside them, so the colour never touches the black. Bits are kept apart and knock out whatever they drift over. In Design Space: three layers to Pen, black 0.4 for the outlines and cracks, black 0.3 (or grey) for the hatching, the colour pen last, Attach, Make.
