Lino decal studio

Ravens, crows and skulls carved like a lino block, then cut from one colour of vinyl. Every mark is a gouge: V-cuts for feathers, U-cups for coverts, a halo cut round each figure so it stands off the frame, and a wobble so the edges look hand-cut. The blade follows one filled outline, so what you see is what stays on the backing.

The decal is one filled path with holes (even-odd), so Design Space reads it as one cut image. The weeding box sits in its own group; in Design Space select both and Attach so they cut together.

computing

How it is made: each figure is drawn as a few hand-placed curves filled black, then carved: the gouge marks are tapered slivers laid along the feathers, bone and bark, and a halo of clear space is cut round every figure before it is laid over a frame or another figure. Carved blocks flip it: the block is solid vinyl and the figure is cut out of it, so its carving comes back as fine vinyl lines. The whole decal is drawn into a fine grid, cleaned to the vinyl rules (gaps closed, slivers opened, specks and pinholes dropped), and traced back into one smooth outline, which is what the blade cuts. All vinyl sizes are ASSUMED until a weeding test is cut. Same seed, same carving.
