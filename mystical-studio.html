Mystical studio

Inktober day 10, mystical. Pick a pair of hands and something mystical for them to do with it: a crystal ball, a tea cup read for its leaves, a fan of tarot cards, or an open palm. Draw the sheets, cut them out and layer them: the backdrop, then the object, then the hands over it, so the hands really do hold, cradle and hover.

The hands are drawn flat, the way a cut-out paper hand would be: fingers bend in the plane of the page. A sleeve is shortened on its own if it would run off the page.

The backdrop
Layer, cut and export

All three sheets share one page and one scale, so each cut-out drops back where it was drawn. On a lower sheet the dashed guide marks where the next piece sits; it is covered once glued. Stack them with foam pads between for depth.

Split export saves one SVG per pen: gold, violet, polish, the shade pen, the line pen, and the cut layer. Each carries four corner ticks at 1 mm. Every piece exports the backdrop, the object and the hands as three sheets, each with its cut line.

computing

How it is made. Every thing on the page is a separate piece of card. The hands are built from tapered capsules (a palm, three bones to a finger, two to a thumb) bent in the plane of the page; their outline, and the cut line round them, are taken from a raster of the union at a quarter of a millimetre, so webs between fingers come out rounded. Pieces are stacked back to front and each hides the lines of what is behind it; on a sheet by itself a piece is drawn whole, since whatever lies on top of it is glued on afterwards. Pen widths and the narrowest cut untested.
