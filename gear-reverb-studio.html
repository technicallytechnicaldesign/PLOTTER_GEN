Gears are exact vectors ported from spur_gear_goblin_v0_6.scad (teeth, web pocket, bore, DIN 6885 keyway), checked against OpenSCAD by 02_WORK/plg-gears/verify_gears.py. Rings come from a 0.2 mm distance field: measured against exact distances they sit 0.04 mm off a true offset on average, 0.16 mm at worst, inside one 0.3 mm pen line; 02_WORK/plg-gears/gear_reverb.py is the exact reference (its seeds lay out differently). In Design Space: set both layers to Pen, black on the 0.4 and grey on the 0.3, Attach, Make.
