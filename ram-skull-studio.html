Ram skull studio

Inktober day 9, ram. A ram's skull on its mount, the horns grown the way real horns grow: a tube swept along a logarithmic spiral, new horn added at the base every year and a ring left between, so the tip is the lamb's horn. Hang a wall of them from lamb to old ram, or lay the years over each other in coloured pens. And if you like, put RAM on the ram.

computing

How the horn is made. Its centreline turns at a steady rate while its radius of curvature shrinks exponentially, which is a logarithmic spiral; it is tilted out from the face and flares sideways along its length, so seen from the front it coils beside the skull. The tube thins at the same rate, so every piece of horn is a scaled copy of every other. Hidden lines are found with a depth buffer: every surface is rasterised at a quarter of a millimetre, and each pen line is kept only where it is the nearest thing to the eye. Pen widths untested.
