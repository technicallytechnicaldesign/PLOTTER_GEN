Forks studio

A mind that can be copied branches like a family tree, uploads slice by slice, and can run as lines of code. Three ways to draw a self that does not stay single, all in profile, all for a pen plotter.

Presets
The sheet

The three drawings have their own groups below. Controls for a drawing you are not looking at stay open, and do nothing until you switch to it. Labels apply to Lineage and Upload.

Lineage

Lineage only. Each head drifts a little from its parent, and the hatching inside it is denser the further it has wandered from the original. The colour line is the original's own line of descent.

Upload

Upload only. In the slice-by-slice method the copy ends up as the whole head, and nothing was ever swapped in one go.

Code

Mind as code only. The lines are written fresh for every seed. One line is picked out in the colour pen.

Drawing
Paper and pens

Three pens: a 0.4 line pen, a 0.3 shade pen and one colour pen. There is no cut layer. The split export saves one SVG per pen, with four corner ticks so every file registers in the same place.

computing

How it is made: the head is one smooth curve through about thirty control points, nudged by seven small numbers (forehead slope, nose length and tip, lips, chin, skull length, neck). A lineage grows from one original, each generation forking, ending or merging at random, and every child carries its parent's seven numbers plus a little drift. Upload hatches the head with offset copies of its own outline, denser at the back of the skull and under the jaw, and the copy is a lattice, a set of voxels or scan lines clipped to the same shape. In the code view each line of text is fitted to the width of the head at its own height, so the letters make the profile. All lettering is single-stroke, so it plots. Pen lengths and the gates above are measured from the drawing itself.
