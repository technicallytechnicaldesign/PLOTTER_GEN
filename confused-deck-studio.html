Confused deck

Inktober day 19, confused: a deck of trading cards, each one drawn as an engineering drawing with its own title block, and each one a small production-floor confusion. Lift detail C and find there is no detail C, lift sheet 1 and find sheet 2 blank, ask the engineer, and spin the tolerance wheel, where half the callouts are real and half are not. The checker's red pen gets crosser card by card.

The cards

Confusion adds red question marks, then a revision cloud, then (from 70) a coffee ring on the odd card. The grumpy faces are always there.

Words

Names go in the sign-off boxes instead of scrawls (the grumpy faces stay). Shortcuts: +/- for ±, [dia] ⌀, [flat] ⏥, [perp] ⊥, [pos] ⌖, [par] ∥, [circ] ○, [conc] ◎, [runout] ↗, [inf] ∞, [deg] °. A letter the pens have no shape for comes out as ?. Presets leave your words alone.

Tolerance spinner

A ; adds a small second line under a callout, and | splits a feature control frame into its boxes ([flat]|0.2 or [pos]|[dia]0.2|A|B).

A paper brad through the hub holds the wheel between the card and its backer; turn it with your thumb in the notch on the left edge. Half the callouts on a sane wheel are ones you will meet at work.

Paper and pens

Split export saves fine (0.3 black), bold (0.5 black), red (the checker's pen), then score, then cut, in job order with matching corner ticks. Flap hinges are scored, everything else is cut.

computing

How it goes together: plot and cut the fronts, then the backers (and the wheel) on a coloured card. Glue each backer behind its front, keeping glue off the flaps. For the spinner, push a paper brad through the front, the wheel and the backer at the hub, and glue the card and backer together round the edges only, so the wheel turns between them. Fold each flap up on its score line once to train it. The brad hole, the flap cuts and the 2 mm bridges are assumptions until the cut calibration sheet has been read.
