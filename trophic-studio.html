Trophic studio

A trophy built out of a food chain. Each tier is a trophic level packed with its creatures, plankton at the foot and the apex predator as the figurine on top, and because only a tenth of the energy climbs each step the tiers really do narrow into a pyramid. The headcounts in the margin are the creatures actually drawn. Export a millimetre SVG with three pen layers for Design Space.

Every creature is packed into its tier without touching another, so the counts in the left margin are honest: they are how many got drawn. The energy column is the ten percent rule, a tenth of the energy surviving each step up. The black pen draws outlines, the 0.3 pen the hatching, the colour pen whatever you pick above. Split export saves one SVG per pen, numbered in plotting order, all with the same four corner ticks so Design Space sizes them identically.

computing

The trophy is drawn in oblique projection: each tier is a block whose front, top and right faces show, the right face hatched, and anything behind something else (the handles behind the top tier, the back columns, a fish tail in the splash, wing feathers under coverts) is trimmed against the outline of what is in front. Creatures are packed by rejection: each one is built, wrapped in a few circles, and dropped at random spots until it fits clear of its neighbours. In Design Space: three layers to Pen, black 0.4 for outlines, black 0.3 (or grey) for the hatching, the colour pen last, Attach, Make.
