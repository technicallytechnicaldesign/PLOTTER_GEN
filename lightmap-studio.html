Lightmap studio

Baking, the rendering kind: a pile of boxes on a floor, their ambient occlusion and sun shadows ray-traced into a lightmap texel by texel, then drawn in hatching twice, on the boxes in the view and flat in the UV atlas the bake was written to, each box unwrapped as a cross with its seams in colour. Export a millimetre SVG with three pen layers for Design Space.

Split export saves one SVG per pen (black outline, grey for the baked light, colour for the UV seams), numbered in plotting order. All carry the same four corner ticks so Design Space sizes them identically.

computing

This is a real bake: every texel of every box face and of the floor casts its rays against the boxes (cosine-weighted and stratified, then lightly denoised), and the light it finds becomes up to three crossing hatch layers. Boxes are drawn back to front so nearer ones hide the lines behind them. In the atlas each box is a cross-shaped net, shelf-packed with the floor at a single texel density, so the hatching there is the same lightmap, laid flat. In Design Space: three layers to Pen, black 0.4 for the edges, black 0.3 (or grey) for the hatching, a colour pen for the seams, Attach, Make.
