How the smell is drawn. The plume is a velocity field: straight up off the source, spreading as it rises, bent by the wind, with curl noise (swirls that never pile up or empty out) faded in with height, so it leaves the source smooth and breaks up higher up. Streamlines are seeded one line-spacing apart from the lines already drawn and stop when they come too close to another (Jobard and Lefer), and the spacing widens with height so the smell thins out. The smell map is the same idea as a concentration: a chain of puffs, each wider and fainter than the last, swirled by the same noise and contoured. Pen widths untested.
