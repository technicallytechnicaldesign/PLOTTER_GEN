Hooray cards

The word, cut through a card covered in one pen pattern, with a second pattern drawn on the coloured paper behind it. Add a middle layer and every letter gets a stepped rim. Hearts, stars and smileys are drawn on, then cut out of the front and the back panel, and everything the blade takes out goes into a printed pocket: the confetti is made from the card. The message goes on its own card in another colour, tucked into corner slits.

Paper and pens

Split export saves black (the 0.4 pen), grey (the 0.3 pen), then score, then cut, in job order with matching corner ticks. Plot each layer on its own paper: the front on pale card, the middle and back on colours.

computing

How it goes together: plot and cut the front, then the layers behind, each on its own paper. Glue the back layer behind the front panel (for three layers, the middle first, on foam tape for real depth). Every letter and confetti shape the blade lifts out of the front and the back panel is confetti, drawn on before it is cut: tip them into the pocket, fold it on its score lines, glue it on, and the small print tells them how many pieces of the card they are holding. Tuck the message card into the four corner slits from inside the card. Stencil bridges keep the middles of the O, A and R. Cut sizes (2 mm bridges, 2.6 mm stencil bridges, 4 mm smallest confetti) are assumptions until the cut calibration sheet has been read.
