Toss studio

A multiple exposure of something thrown: the same coin, die or graduation cap caught at several moments along its arc, spinning as it goes, with a dashed trail, shadows on the ground and the last one landed. Day 12 of Inktober, "toss".

Paper

Split export saves one SVG per pen in plotting order: the thin pen first, then the black line last so it sits on top. Each file carries four corner ticks so they all land in the same place.

computing

How it is made: each thing is a small 3D model (a disc, a cube, a board on a skull cap) turned by one rotation matrix per moment and projected flat, so the pips, the star and the reeded coin edge all foreshorten correctly. Later moments sit in front of earlier ones and cut their lines. The spin runs down to nothing at the landing, so the last one comes to rest flat.
