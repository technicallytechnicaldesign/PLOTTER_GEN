Orrery studio

Every seed grows a new star system: a star with its own corona, three to ten planets on orbits spaced like the Titius-Bode rule, rings, moons, an asteroid belt, sometimes a comet or a second star. Draw it from above, tilted, as a textbook plate, as a brass orrery on a stack of gears, or as a close-up portrait of one ringed giant. Export a millimetre SVG with pen layers for Design Space.

Split export saves one SVG per pen (key, shade, colour), numbered in plotting order. Every file carries the same four corner ticks so Design Space sizes them identically: place each file at the same spot on the mat, swap pens between runs, and leave the paper on the mat.

computing

The generator lays out planets with gaps that grow outward, in the spirit of the Titius-Bode rule, then gives each one a kind from its distance: rocky inside the frost line, gas and ice giants outside it. Every planet is a shaded sphere. The night side is hatched at the pitch you set, rocky planets carry craters that squash toward the limb, gas giants carry wavy latitude bands and a storm, and ice giants lie on their sides. Rings are drawn as tilted ellipses with the back half hidden by the planet and the front half hiding the planet, plus the ring's shadow on the disc. All hidden lines are removed by depth, so planets hide orbit lines and each other, and the star hides the far side of orbits. The brass view reuses the involute teeth of gear-weave-studio.html for a stack of meshing gears at the base. In Design Space: every layer to Pen, black 0.4 for the key layer, black 0.3 for the shade layer, and any colour pen for the colour layer (rings, comet tails, and orbits if you turn that on), Attach, Make.
